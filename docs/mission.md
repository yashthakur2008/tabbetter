# Mission and team

TabBetter is a nonprofit initiative helping speech and debate participants spend less time finding tournament information and more time preparing, competing and judging.

**Yash Thakur — Co-founder**

The project improves the presentation and organization of tournament discovery, public entry/judge lists, schedules and published pairings. It operates independently of Tabroom and the National Speech & Debate Association.

The domain tabbetter.com is owned by the project founder; hosting/DNS connection is tracked as launch work. Project impact should be described using observed usage and outcomes as they become available, rather than assumed adoption or affiliation.
